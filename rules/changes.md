# What changed

- 2026-11-22: The reference has a section on the ladder: what a ranked game is, what moves a rating and by how much, that a draw counts as half a win and a voided game never counts, what the ± says and when a rating is provisional. The Mutation and Conditions cards are shorter, with no rule changed (ADR 0124, ADR 0176).
- 2026-11-18: Three spells take their locked names: Whirlwind is Groundbreaker, Claim is Seize, Soul Feast is Devour. No rule moved.
- 2026-11-18: The mutation card no longer says a capstone gives an initiative bonus: a capstone gives a passive in place of spells and an initiative bonus (ADR 0101).
- 2026-11-18: The examples state the damage Blood Price deals its own caster (ADR 0031).
- 2026-11-18: The shared mutation's card says "For every creature of the team", the book's word (ADR 0114).
- 2026-11-17: A Stage is written in Roman numerals, Stage I to Stage IV, as the table and the field guide write it.
- 2026-11-16: The official rulebook is the app's edition. A creature chooses its spell when its turn comes. Every number follows the table's rule set and content (ADR 0112, ADR 0188).
- 2026-11-16: The tutorials' cards come from this book and link to their sections. The Health card says healing stops at the starting Health, and the Defense card that its buffs count for 10 at most (ADR 0076, ADR 0187).
- 2026-11-16: Archmage's passive goes to every creature of its team, once for each copy, and a copy keeps giving after the creature that acquired it is defeated (ADR 0114).
- 2026-11-16: The book reads in French too, with the same sections, numbers and cards; English and Français switch at the same section (ADR 0193).
