# Scene paintings for the app and showcase

These ten WebP sources keep the full framing of the selected paintings. The placements below are starting points. Open a painting to inspect the detail at full size.

| Painting | Possible placement | Detail to keep when cropping |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [The broken charge](elemental-charge.webp) · 16:9 | Showcase introduction to combat or the spell catalogue. Set the caption below the action. | For Incinerate, keep both the Cataclysm’s mouth and the impact on the Colossus’s shoulder. The ice around the planted foot and the lightning above the shoulder can each stand alone in a smaller spell tile. |
| [Parasite beneath the slate](parasite-under-slate.webp) · 16:9 | Bestiary teaser or an encounter reveal. | Keep the dark mouth to the upper left of the bright claw. The claw draws the eye first, then the teeth become visible. A close crop of the claw alone works as a smaller teaser. |
| [Blightweaver under pressure](blightweaver-pressure-sac.webp) · 3:1 | Mutation detail header, with the name on the dark shoulder at left. | In a compact card, frame the amber sac and its vent. A wider crop can also reveal the eye through the smoke at the far right. |
| [Ironhide’s scar](ironhide-scar.webp) · 3:1 | Player profile banner with the name on the left. | For a portrait tile, take the right end where the diagonal scar crosses the plates above the eye. |
| [The Brute’s moult](brute-moult.webp) · 3:1 | Header above the mutation choices. The empty left side can carry the heading. | Keep the shed shell and fresh print together to suggest what walked away. The print alone could mark a completed evolution. |
| [Predator at rest](predator-at-rest.webp) · 16:9 | Lobby backdrop, with the main action in the shadow on the left. | Anchor a narrow crop on the raised skull and amber shoulder. A lower strip can pick up the foreclaws and their reflection. |
| [After Crushing Stomp](crushing-stomp-aftermath.webp) · 3:1 | Match results, with the outcome on the quiet right side. | The three marks and the slab shedding dust are grouped on the left. That end also works as an illustration for the spell. |
| [Warped listening](warped-listening.webp) · 16:9 | Opponent-waiting screen, with the status in the upper-left archway. | Keep the claw tip, the approaching ripple and the sliver of water between them. A shallow crop along the waterline retains the gesture. |
| [Predator and Deathstalker at the pool](predator-and-deathstalker.webp) · 16:9 | Showcase section on mutation paths. | Keep both lowered heads and the Deathstalker’s wide scythes in the full scene. For two companion cards, crop one creature per bank and keep the waterline level across the pair. |
| [Latch beneath the arch](latch-under-the-arch.webp) · 16:9 | Latch spell detail or a Predator feature in the bestiary. | The full view leads from the three claws to the eye on the right. For a small spell tile, frame one hooked claw together with the crumbling stone it grips. |

For a placement that ships, use the [page picture workflow](../../../page-pictures.md) to derive the sizes that surface needs. At phone width, check the crop with the real heading or controls in place.
