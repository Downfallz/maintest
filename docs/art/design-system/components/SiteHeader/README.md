# SiteHeader

The site's header: the rune, the name in `wordmark`, the navigation, the bell and the account.

- The rune is `assets/Logos/brand-mark.svg` (bone, no square) at 22×34px; 18×28px under 700px.
- The name is uppercase `wordmark` (17px, .14em) in `bone`; 13px/.1em under 700px, 11px/.06em under 360px.
- The current page (`aria-current="page"`) and hover: label `bone`, a 2px `gold` underline. At 1279px and below the navigation folds into a menu on `panel` with grain, and the mark becomes a 2px left border on `accent-wash`.
- A bone hairline fading into `line-soft` runs under the header.
- In a match the header folds into the right end of the round's bar.
