# Scene

A painting of the scenes' library in the table, in one of three forms (`sceneElement` of `table/picture.js`). The card draws a stand-in gradient where the painting goes: the paintings are served by the table, not by this page.

- **Head**: a 3:1 band across the top of a window, a sheet or a page, no taller than 120px, and 64px on a screen under 600px tall. The `eyebrow` in `ember` and the title in `bone` sit on the painting's darker side (`data-side="start"` or `"end"`) over a scrim from `soot` that fades out across the band.
- **Ground**: a card's or a dialog's painting, cut to its box and dimmed under `soot` from 84% to 92%, its words on it. Under less data it is not drawn, and the card keeps its slate.
- **Plate**: a `figure`, the painting whole with its `alt`, its words under it in `muted`.
- Words on a painting hold 4.5:1 against the lightest pixel under them; the journey walk measures it. A head's or a ground's painting is a decoration and takes no `alt`.
- A head never pushes a window's filled action off a 320 by 568 screen.
