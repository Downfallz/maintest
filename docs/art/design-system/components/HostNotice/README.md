# HostNotice

The operator's notice (ADR 0177): one line under the header, `bone` on `panel` at 70% with a 2px `gold` rule at its left.

- The consumer provides one or two sentences; long words wrap.
- Never over the board. One notice at a time.
- On a phone's home it lies under the header, over the top of the featured space's painting, on `panel` at 94%, so
  the space keeps its whole screen.
