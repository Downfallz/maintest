Everyone starts at 1500 ± 350: a guess. Each ranked game moves your rating by how surprising the result was, and shrinks the ±, how sure the ladder is of it.

What provisional means

Provisional means your rating is still being found: its ± is above 110. It usually takes 10 to 15 ranked games to settle, fewer against settled players. Until then you are listed after the settled players, whatever your rating, and cannot hold a top place. A long break makes a rating less sure again, a little each day.

Games against a bot, on a shared device, or opened as unranked do not count; your record against each bot is kept apart.
