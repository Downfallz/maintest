        Your data

        What we keep

        Your player name, an internal id, and the number Google gives your account, so you are you when you come back. Not your email, your real name or your photo.

        Who your friends are, and who asked whom, until one of you removes the other. If you ask to be notified on a device, where to reach that browser, until you stop it or sign out everywhere.

        The games your seats play are recorded under the internal id, to replay them and to improve the bots.

        Delete your account any time from your name at the top: your name is freed, your seats in unfinished games are offered to other players, and finished games keep only the id.
